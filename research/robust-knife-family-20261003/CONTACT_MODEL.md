The installed IsaacGym Preview4 release notes at docs/_sources/release-notes.rst.txt:26 specify average friction combination for all shapes. Official PhysX PxCombineMode reference: https://nvidia-omniverse.github.io/PhysX/physx/5.1.0/_build/physx/latest/struct_px_combine_mode.html . The training family authors hand friction0.7–1.3 and knife1.5–3.0, yielding arithmetic-average pair1.1–2.15. These are engineering hypotheses, not calibrated skin/knife coefficients. Both authored coefficients are saved by environment slot at reset. Normal net-force/contact and relative velocity are diagnostics, not exact thumb–slider pair forces. Run resistance is dissipative; detent/start terms are bounded conservative potentials, so local positive power on descending a potential is not an actuator aid. Source scripts keep slider drive stiffness0 and never send a position command to move it.
